## Context

[Placeholder] Small clinics still schedule patients by phone and paper. Receptionists juggle
calls, confirmations and last-minute cancellations — and empty slots cost money.

## Problem

[Placeholder] Clinics were losing revenue to no-shows and staff time to manual confirmations.

- ~20% of appointments were no-shows
- Patients could only book during business hours
- Clinic owners had no visibility of occupancy

## My role

[Placeholder] I led research, UX and UI, and built the functional MVP in FlutterFlow
so pilot clinics could use it in real conditions.

## Process

[Placeholder] **Research →** shadowed receptionists in 3 clinics.
**Design →** designed patient booking (mobile-first) and the clinic dashboard.
**Build →** functional MVP in FlutterFlow with automated WhatsApp reminders.
**Measure →** compared no-show rates before and after in pilot clinics.

## Key decisions

1. **WhatsApp over email.** That's where patients actually answer.
2. **One-tap confirm or reschedule.** Reducing friction beat adding features.
3. **Occupancy dashboard first.** Owners buy what shows them lost revenue.

## Outcome

[Placeholder — replace with real numbers]

- **−35%** no-shows in pilot clinics
- **60%** of bookings made outside business hours
- **5 hours/week** saved per receptionist

## Learnings

[Placeholder] Shipping a functional MVP early revealed edge cases (family bookings, recurring
sessions) that no prototype test would have surfaced.
