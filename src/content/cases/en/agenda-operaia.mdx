## Context

Small clinics run on a mix of Google Calendar, WhatsApp and paper forms. Each professional has their own calendar, reception can't see everyone at once, and patient information arrives scattered.

## Problem

- No single view of the team's day, even though everyone already uses Google Calendar
- Triage information (complaints, photos) arrives by message and gets lost
- Staff jump between phone, laptop and paper — none of them "the system"

## My role

Product design and full-stack build: flows, UI, and the implementation — a Vue 3 PWA and an Express + Prisma API with Google Calendar integration, push notifications and multi-clinic administration.

## Process

**Research →** mapped the clinic's day: confirm, receive, attend, record, remind.
**Design →** a dashboard centered on *now*, a team agenda that merges every professional's calendar, and a patient record with triage.
**Build →** an installable PWA (desktop, Android and iPhone) on top of the calendars clinics already use — no migration.
**Measure →** a "team today" report shows confirmed appointments and time recorded per professional against the previous week.

## Key decisions

1. **Build on Google Calendar, don't replace it.** Clinics keep their calendars; the app adds the team view, patients and records on top.
2. **Install, don't download.** A PWA gives a home-screen app on any device without app stores — and one-tap calls to patients.
3. **Triage before the visit.** A private link lets the patient send complaints and photos in advance, which land in the chart.

## Outcome

- Team agenda with the next appointment, monthly calendar and today's list
- Patient records with triage links, visits, treatments and imported patient lists
- Reminders 15 minutes before each appointment, public booking page and per-clinic branding
- A platform admin to create clinics, plans and features

## Learnings

The fastest way to get a clinic to adopt new software is to **not ask them to leave the tools they trust**. Integrating with Google Calendar removed the biggest objection.
